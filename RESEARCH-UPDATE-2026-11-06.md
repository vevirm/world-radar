### Other results

**T13 (2020 to 2026).** The original paper analysed conditional 2050 technical emissions possibilities and an illustrative 2030 new-light-duty-vehicle Mix path. Eurostat reports 1.89 million newly registered BEV passenger cars in 2025 and a BEV passenger-car fleet of 7.59 million (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260731-1). EEA’s 2026 inventory synthesis reports that road-transport GHG emissions in 2024 were still 24% above 1990 (https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/analysis/publications/key-trends-and-drivers-in-ghg-emissions-in-the-eu). This is not a like-for-like back-test: car sales, vehicle stock, fleet traffic, upstream electricity, GHG and tank-to-wheel CO2 differ. The Commission’s December 2025 proposed change to 2035 car standards is a **proposal**, not enacted law (https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/transport-decarbonisation/road-transport/cars-and-vans_en).

**T04.** EEA EU land-sector sink: historical 2023 = 198 MtCO2e; preliminary 2024 = 212. Two 2030 scenario results, not observed 2030 outcomes: with existing measures = 183, with planned additional measures = 233; legal target = 310 (https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/analysis/indicators/greenhouse-gas-emissions-from-land). The older 2017 441 Mt additional climate-smart forestry mitigation is a different system boundary.

**T05.** EASA’s 2 November 2026 report corrigendum clarifies EU SAF-production/import interpretation without changing 2025 EU-airport supply 1.1 Mt/2.79% (https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/general-publications/refueleu-aviation-annual-technical-report-2026). The EASA dashboard distinguishes 90.5 kt advanced aviation biofuels, 80.2% of SAF from used cooking oil, and 85.1% feedstock sourced outside the EU (https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/refueleu-aviation/annual-technical-report/dashboard-refueleu-aviation-2026). Feedstock origin is not the geography of finished fuel imports; the 2018 renewable-jet-fuel scenario uses a narrower object.
