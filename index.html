Bibliography

Original research publications and later evidence, when verified
    Method. Include research from a rolling 15-year maximum, initially prioritising 5–10-year-old studies (about 2016–2021); verified findings must also be at least six months old. Older exceptions within 15 years and especially significant newer papers remain eligible. EU institutions, independent think tanks, universities, international organisations, research institutes and EU-funded project consortia are eligible only when the work actually concerns EU/European futures. Two evidence stages are deliberately separate: research leads are discovered from original DOI records and available abstracts (including paywalled journals); a missing database abstract is labelled as such and they are not accepted full studies. Only verified findings require original full text, substantive analysis, identified foresight methods, references/sources, and an identifiable future-facing claim. A thin brief cannot pass, whatever its nominal page count. This is a conservative automated screening gate, not peer review. Scenarios are possibilities, not predictions. Retrospective judgements require separately dated evidence. Unassessed is not “wrong”.
    Monitored publishers: EU institutions · Independent think tanks · Universities · Research institutes · International organisations · EU-funded research consortia
