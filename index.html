04 · Research method

Not every future is a prediction.

World Radar includes conditional projections, qualitative scenarios, warnings and strategic questions. A scenario's value cannot be measured by a simple right-or-wrong score.

We do not award outcomes based on search-result similarity, policy announcements or a model published afterward. An investigated absence of a defensible link is recorded and explained. An original never checked remains explicitly unassessed; we do not conflate those states.

  1. Start with the historical source. Identify what was actually anticipated, and under which assumptions.
  2. Find dated later evidence. Prefer measurements, evaluations and documented developments addressing the same issue.
  3. Separate action from impact. A law or announced project is not proof that an envisioned future has happened.
  4. Explain the relationship. Record similarities, differences, what cannot be established and continuing relevance.
  5. Document unsuccessful research too. A no-link finding needs an attributable search trail across distinct evidence types; a missing OpenAlex match alone does not qualify.
